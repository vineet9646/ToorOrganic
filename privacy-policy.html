Privacy Policy

Pure Trust, Committed to Purity

Last Updated: June 2026

At Toor Organic, we are deeply committed to protecting the privacy and security of our valued customers. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, use, and protect your personal information when you interact with our luxury digital store and explore our premium Basmati rice varieties.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver a smooth, tailored shopping experience, we secure distinct datasets from our visitors:

  • Identity Details: Full name, billing address, secure delivery metrics, and direct contact variables (phone number, email).
  • Transaction Data: Specific details regarding product parameters, orders, and interaction sequences processing through our server framework.
  • Technical Logins: IP tracking matrices, core browser behavior, browser dimensions, and standard device variables tracked during session navigation.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your digital identity metrics are strictly implemented to preserve premium logistical security bounds:

  • To process, package, dispatch, and fulfill your premium organic product orders smoothly.
  • To initialize direct client communication, manage verification tokens, or handle continuous customer care tracking parameters.
  • To serve customized marketing materials, seasonal harvest drops, and promotional alerts specifically tailored to your brand preference profiles.

3. Security Frameworks

We deploy high-end cryptographic system layers (SSL/TLS validation handshakes) across our host platforms to insulate transactions from digital vectors. Access privileges are strictly compartmentalized internally, ensuring only verified administrative lines interact with client accounts.

4. Cookie Management

Our infrastructure uses cookie identifiers to adapt visual elements based on screen scales and preserve items inside active shopping cart arrays. You can change browser permissions to drop incoming cookie packets at any point, though it may alter your smooth tracking flow on our website.
