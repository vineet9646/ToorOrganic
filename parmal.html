Elite Parmal Basmati Rice

Quality in every grain, trust in every meal

Elite Parmal rice is a premium non-basmati rice known for its clean, slender grains and soft texture after cooking. It cooks evenly, remains light and fluffy, and pairs well with everyday Indian meals such as dal, curry, vegetables, and pulao. Its mild natural aroma and pleasant taste make it a practical choice for both daily home cooking and family meals. Parmal rice is also valued for its good water absorption, which helps it take on the flavors of spices and gravies while keeping the grains separate.
